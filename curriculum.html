The Academic Pathway

One clear route, Year 1 to Year 13 — built on self-discovery

We follow the British National Curriculum from the very first year, but our approach goes further: every phase is designed to help a child understand how they learn and what they're genuinely good at, before guiding them toward a worldwide accredited Pearson Edexcel IGCSE and A Level certificate.

Adaptive Learning

Learning that adapts to the child, not the other way around

Every student learns differently, so our adaptive learning model adjusts pace, practice and support to how each child actually learns best. Behind every live lesson sits a system that notices where a student is stuck, offers targeted extra practice, and gives parents an honest, easy-to-read picture of progress and emerging talents — in one place.

  • Personalised, adaptive practice after every topic
  • Talent and strength mapping alongside academic grades
  • Plain-language progress dashboards for parents
  • Early alerts to teachers when a learner needs support

How We Deliver Lessons

One simple rhythm — before, during and after every lesson

Live teaching sits at the centre of NoorBridge, supported by a clear, predictable routine so students always know what to do next and parents always know what's happening.

🗓️

Before Class

  1. Check the timetableEvery learner has a personalised calendar showing upcoming live lessons at a glance.
  2. Access lesson resourcesReading materials and preparation resources are ready in the dashboard ahead of each topic.
🎥

During Class

  1. Join with one clickLearners join their scheduled live class directly from the dashboard, right on time.
  2. Learn in real timeLive teaching with a subject specialist and classmates — not pre-recorded video.
  3. Ask & interact liveQuestions, discussion and live chat keep every learner actively involved, not just watching.
📝

After Class

  1. Submit homeworkAssignments are completed and uploaded straight through the student dashboard.
  2. Rewatch anytimeEvery lesson is recorded, so revision or catching up after an absence is never a problem.
  3. Review marks & feedbackGrades and teacher feedback appear as soon as work is marked — no waiting.
🏅

Progress & Recognition

  1. Track attendanceA clear, always-visible attendance record for both learners and parents.
  2. Earn recognitionDigital badges and rewards celebrate effort, attendance and achievement along the way.
